Business Side of online streaming platforms


Revenue Models

Streaming services earn income in different way:


Source(https://www.degonline.org/9-people-who-are-building-the-future-of-online-video/.com)

Subscription Video on Demand (SVOD)

: Users pay a monthly subscription fee to access content.Examples include Netflix, Disney+, and Showmax.

Advertising Video on Demand (AVOD): Viewers can watch content for free, but advertisements generate revenue for the platform.

Hybrid Model: Some platforms combine subscription fees with advertising, giving users multiple pricing options.

Creator Support: Live-streaming platforms such as YouTube Live and Twitch allow audiences to support creators through subscriptions, donations, or virtual gifts.

Additional Income: Some companies also earn revenue through merchandise sales, ticket sales, or premium services.

Major Costs for Streaming Companies


Operating a streaming platform requires significant investment: Producing or licensing movies, TV shows, and live sports rights.
Maintaining Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) to deliver videos quickly and reliably.
Paying for servers, cloud storage, and the internet bandwidth to stream content worldwide.

How Streaming Technology Works



Source(https://www.lifewire.com/internet-streaming-how-it-works-1999513) 		A Content Delivery Network (CDN) stores copies of videos on servers around the world, reducing loading times and improving playback.
Video codecs such as H.264, H.265 (HEVC), and AV1 compress video files so they use less data while maintaining quality.
Adaptive Bitrate Streaming (ABR) automatically changes video quality depending on the user's internet speed to reduce buffering.(J Holland, 2022)


Advantages and Disadvantages of Streaming


Advantages


  • Users can watch content instantly without downloading files.
  • Streaming platforms offer large libraries of movies, TV shows, music, and live broadcasts.
  • Live streaming allows creators and audiences to interact in real time.

Disadvantages

  • Streaming consumes a significant amount of internet data.
  • A stable and fast internet connection is required for smooth playback.
  • High-quality streaming may increase data costs.


    • Streaming in South Africa

    Challenges

    Mobile data remains expensive, making high-quality streaming costly for many users. Power outages (load shedding) often interrupt streaming, leading users to download content for offline viewing. Although fibre internet is expanding, many South Africans still rely on mobile data. Popular Streaming Platforms Showmax is popular for local entertainment and sports content. YouTube is widely used because much of its content is free and supports offline viewing. Netflix remains one of the most popular subscription streaming services. TikTok Live has become a popular platform for live content, especially among younger users.

    Impact


    The growth of online streaming has significantly changed how people access entertainment and information. It offers convenience, huge content libraries, and real-time interaction between creators and audiences. However, it also raises concerns about high data consumption dependence on stable internet, subscription costs, and digital fatigue from excessive screen time.

    Phase 2 Data Processing and Calculations


    To address the findings from the questionnaire, the survey data was processed and presented using spreadsheet and database tools.

    Spreadsheet charts: Bar charts and pie charts were created to visually show platform preferences like Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, and Disney+ among high school students.

    Formulas: COUNTIF was used to count how many students prefer to share their online streaming account.AVERAGE was used to calculate the average age of the high school students.

    Database Queries: Queries were run to compare streaming habits by age group and device type, (for example smartphone, laptop, smart TV and tablet).

    Database Report: A report was created to summarize key trends in local streaming behaviour, such as device preferences and streaming platforms.
